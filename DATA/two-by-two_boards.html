Two by Two — Tier A boards

Every 2×2 the Tier A pairs can make: 29 artist/subject pairings, 73 grids once each cell's alternatives are counted. Rows are one artist, columns one subject. A mockup for judging the boards by eye — nothing here is connected to the game. Subjects were matched on title text, so this page is where that guess gets checked against the pictures. Cards link to the Getty object page.
